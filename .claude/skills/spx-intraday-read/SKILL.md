# SPX Intraday Read

Times in screenshots are SGT: 21:30 = 8:30 AM ET, 22:00 = 10:00 AM ET (adjust by one hour when US daylight saving changes).

## For a data screenshot
1. Read actual vs consensus vs prior; flag revisions.
2. Verdict: hot / in-line / cool, and the likely Fed read (next-meeting odds direction).
3. 10-year reaction vs the 5.00% pivot → implied SPX direction.
4. Updated must-hold and resistance, and whether the pre-market bias still stands.
5. Reminder of the 15-30 min wait rule on data days.

## For live SPX / 10-year levels
- Where price sits vs the levels table from the pre-market analysis.
- Is it approaching support/resistance (don't chase) or at first touch of resistance (fade candidate)?
- Any change to scenario odds; state new odds briefly.
- Time check: flag when within 30 min of 3:30 PM ET exit.

## For "any bullish/bearish news?"
Search now (last 1-2 hours): Fed speakers, megacap/AI headlines, oil/Iran, Treasury moves, notable movers. Return two short lists — Bullish / Bearish — each item with source and time, then a one-line net read.

Keep replies short; this is used mid-session.
