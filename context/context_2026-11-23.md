## How we work

- **Daily prompt:** a 0DTE SPX pre-market analysis with the current SPX level.
- **During the session:** data screenshots (times in SGT, so 21:30/22:00 = 8:30/10:00 AM ET), live SPX and 10-year levels, and "any bullish/bearish news?"
- **Analysis format:** recap, catalysts, data read, levels table, must-hold, major resistance, scenario odds, trade bias.
- **Limits:** no live GEX, X/Twitter or Fear & Greed data; no video; search snippets sometimes conflict. On bond-market holidays, the cash 10-year doesn't update (use Treasury futures instead).
- **Time zones:** SGT = ET + 13 hours until Mar 14, 2027 (US daylight saving starts), then ET + 12.

## Trading rules

- **Timing:** wait 15-30 minutes after the open on data or volatile days, and a full 30 minutes on big gaps, expiration days or a VIX above 30. Be flat before FOMC minutes or a Fed chair speech. Exit by 3:30 PM ET; the last 30 minutes can whip on expiration and month-end flows.
- **Size:** tiered by VIX: up to 30% when the VIX is below 20, 15-20% at 20-30, \~10% above 30. Cap the dollar loss per trade at \[your number, e.g. 1% of account\].
- **Profit-taking:** take half at the first target and move the stop to breakeven.
- **Stops:** no re-entry after two stops.
- **Entries:** don't chase into support or resistance. Fade the first touch of resistance. Range trades target the other side of the range. Enter on confirmation (a reclaim of the level), not by catching a falling market.
- **Data-day trigger:** use the 10-year yield: 4.95% or below is supportive, 5.15% or above is bearish (5.00% is still the psychological line).

## Lessons from recent sessions

- [ ] **SPX is \~40% tech and megacaps.** Nvidia, Microsoft, Apple, Alphabet, Amazon, Meta, Broadcom and SMH drive it more than the Dow does.
- [ ] **The first disappointment gets treated as company-specific; the second confirms a trend.**
- [ ] **Catalyst gaps hold; non-catalyst gaps fade.** Nov 2, 4, 18 and 19 faded; Nov 23 (Huang's return) held.
- [ ] **After a breakdown, the prior close is the first resistance.** Nov 17's high stalled right at Nov 16's close.
- [ ] **Good news being sold is a warning sign.** Nvidia's beat and the ANT pop both faded: positioning and the trend dominate.
- [ ] **Stocks and yields falling together means risk-off,** not rate relief (Nov 20).
- [ ] **A 1% Nvidia move is worth \~5 SPX points.** Use it to attribute moves.
